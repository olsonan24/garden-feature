# Keyword harvester and negator playbook

Playbook version: `1.0.0`

## Harvest

A harvest review needs repeat order evidence in STR. The current deterministic rule requires two or more orders and sales above zero. Inventory must be known and safe before the language can suggest conservative scaling. Campaign structure and current target coverage should be checked with the Targeting Report.

## Negative review

A negative review needs STR evidence of material spend and clicks without orders. The current threshold is at least $15 spend and 8 clicks. This is not an automatic negative: relevance, match type, placement, conversion/offer issues, and the account's approved risk tolerance still require operator review.

SQP and Cerebro may expand investigation, but cannot prove waste or create a hard negative. Every recommendation is editable, manager-approved, evidence-linked, and non-executable until an authorized Amazon Ads integration exists.
