# Listing and offer diagnostic playbook

Playbook version: `1.0.0`

The listing module is separate from PPC. Traffic with persistently weak conversion can reveal a constraint in price, offer, content, reviews, availability, or product-market fit; PPC is not presented as the cure.

The current deterministic signal requires a persisted Business Report by Child ASIN and at least 40 sessions with conversion below 2%. Week-over-week conversion decline is separately identified when current conversion is at least 25% below a comparable prior week.

Evidence contains sessions, conversion, period, SKU/ASIN, source report, and calculation. Exact listing content, price history, coupon state, buy box, review causes, or creative quality remain unavailable unless imported or saved with a source. Recommendations require PSM approval and are diagnostic only.
